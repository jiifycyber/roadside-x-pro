# AI Safety and Governance

1. AI recommendations are advisory unless an owner enables a narrow automation rule.
2. Human approval remains required for pricing, dispatch exceptions, payments,
   employee discipline, customer-facing messages and partner disputes.
3. Fraud/anomaly alerts identify records for review; they are not accusations.
4. Store prompts, recommendations, approvals and actions in an audit trail.
5. Enforce company isolation and role-based permissions in the backend.
6. Provide a non-AI fallback for all essential dispatch operations.
